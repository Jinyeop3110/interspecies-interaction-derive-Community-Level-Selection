## Panel b: reproduction

Exact.

| | Published | This code |
|---|---|---|
| Nutr− | 44 ± 2% | 43.8 ± 2.1% |
| Base | 51 ± 5% | 51.1 ± 5.1% |
| Nutr+ | 67 ± 4% | 66.9 ± 4.2% |
| *n* | — | 18 / 17 / 18 |

Two selection choices matter, and both were wrong in an earlier version of this
script:

- the panel covers the **12-species parental communities only**, not all three
  richness classes;
- the mean is taken over individual cultures, not over communities after
  averaging their replicates.

Pooling all richness classes and averaging within communities gives 42.4 / 59.6
/ 66.7% — the Base value drifts about nine points while the other two barely
move, which is what made the discrepancy look medium-specific rather than
structural.

## Panel c: blocker

Panel c regresses event PDI against the outcome of the pairwise competition
between the two parental communities' dominant species. Those assay results are
not among the processed tables, so the panel cannot be regenerated from the
archive. PDI itself is available from `coalescence.outcomes.outcome_table`.

Two filters are applied in the original and must be ported with it:

- a mixing filter, keeping only events with `u² + v² > 0.5` (excluding
  Restructuring);
- a bistability filter on the monoculture reference measurements.
